# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Patients**: People seeking medical consultation remotely. They may be anxious, in discomfort, or caring for family members. Primary tasks: finding a doctor, waiting in queue, chatting with doctor, consulting AI health assistant.

**Doctors**: Medical professionals managing multiple simultaneous consultations. Primary tasks: monitoring queue, conducting text-based consultations, managing workload.

## Product Purpose

之江小智 is an online medical consultation platform that connects patients with doctors through text-based consultation, supplemented by an AI health assistant for triage and basic guidance. Success means patients get timely medical advice without visiting a hospital, and doctors can efficiently manage their consultation workload.

## Brand Personality

Modern, clean, precise — like a well-designed medical device interface. Three words: **precise, calm, capable**.

- **Precise**: Every element has purpose. No decorative fluff.
- **Calm**: Reduces patient anxiety through clean layouts and reassuring visual feedback.
- **Capable**: Feels technologically advanced (AI-powered) but never flashy or gimmicky.

Tone: professional but warm. Never clinical/cold, never cartoonish/friendly. The right balance: a competent doctor who explains things clearly.

## Anti-references

- Traditional hospital information systems — dense tables, harsh blue-white color schemes, small fonts, overwhelming information density
- Old-school medical software — dated gradients, heavy borders, cluttered dashboards
- Generic SaaS dashboards — hero-metric templates, identical card grids, side-stripe borders

## Design Principles

1. **Reduce cognitive load**: Each screen should have one clear primary action. Strip away anything that doesn't serve the patient's or doctor's immediate task.
2. **Clarity over decoration**: Use whitespace, typography, and hierarchy to guide attention. Never rely on decorative elements to communicate importance.
3. **Calm technology**: The interface stays out of the way. Transitions are smooth but fast, feedback is immediate but subtle, errors are helpful without being alarming.
4. **Mobile-first empathy**: Patients may access from phones in stressful situations. Every interaction must be thumb-friendly and forgiving.
5. **Consistent design language**: A unified visual system across patient-facing and doctor-facing interfaces — they're different views of the same product, not separate products.
6. **Accessibility by default**: WCAG-compliant contrast, readable font sizes, clear focus indicators.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG compliance. Adequate color contrast, clear focus states, readable font sizes (min 14px body text).
